# Finished trips, finished finances

A trip that's over isn't always done. A balance still open, an overpayment to refund, and the big one: a deposit waiting to go back to your customer. Returning deposits is finally here, with one click, in full or partly if the boat came back with a scratch. The new next-step card on the booking's Finance tab shows exactly what's left and hands you the button to handle it.

With the new Booking wrap-up page you settle the whole day in one sitting: every finished booking that still needs something in one list, handled one by one or in batch. Bookings that deserve a closer look get flagged automatically, so nothing slips through.

## Other updates

- **Collect a payment, your way.** One Collect payment button on the booking now covers it all: charge the card on file, take it on your terminal, or send a payment link. Pick the method, the rest is pre-filled.
- **Margins, per booking.** The turnaround margins from your schedule are now editable on each booking. That one charter that needs extra cleaning time gets it, without touching your schedule.
- **Flags fly on the planning.** Flagged bookings show a pennant right on the booking bar, so they catch your eye without opening anything.
- **Find any booking.** Search now looks across all your bookings instead of just the current view, and when a filtered search comes up empty, one click searches without filters.
- **Quieter inboxes, if you want.** You can now turn off booking-update emails to customers for your whole account.
- **A separate technical contact.** Point technical notifications at your tech person instead of the general inbox.
- **Stronger sign-in security.** Two-factor authentication got a serious round of hardening behind the scenes.
- Various performance and reliability improvements under the hood.

## Bugfixes

- Add-on orders of cancelled bookings are visible again, so you can see what was ordered before the plans changed.
- The signed-documents icon found its way back to the bookings overview.
- Fixed an issue where add-ons lingered on cancelled bookings.
