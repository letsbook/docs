# Payment terminals

Charge a booking right at the counter. A remaining balance, a walk-in, or a drink someone grabs on the way out: add it to the booking and tap it off on a card reader, so nobody gets sent off to pay somewhere else.

## Get a card reader

Order a reader from your payment provider:

- [Mollie card readers](https://www.mollie.com/products/pos-payments)
- [Stripe card readers](https://stripe.com/terminal/devices)

With Mollie you can also use Tap to Pay straight from your own phone, no extra hardware needed.

## Set up your terminals

The terminals page shows the readers that are paired with your Mollie or Stripe account. Pairing itself happens at your payment provider; Let's Book syncs the list from there, so you never set up a reader twice.

<Button href="https://dashboard.letsbook.app/integrations/terminals">
    Open terminal settings →
</Button>

1. Pair your reader in your Mollie or Stripe dashboard
2. For Mollie, click Connect terminals on the [integrations page](https://dashboard.letsbook.app/integrations/psp) to give Let's Book access
3. Click Sync now, and every reader on your provider account appears in the list
4. Click a terminal to open its settings

Each terminal has two settings of its own:

- **Use with Let's Book** decides whether the reader is offered as a payment option when charging
- **Used at** limits the reader to specific docks, so staff only see the readers at their location. Leave it on All docks if one reader serves your whole operation

The list shows each terminal's live status (Online, Offline, or Pending), synced from your provider.

## Charge a booking

1. Open the booking and go to the Finance tab
2. Click Collect payment and pick Terminal
3. Check the amount and description, both pre-filled with what's open
4. Select your reader and hand it to the customer

You follow the payment live in the modal: connecting, waiting for the tap, paid. The payment lands on the booking like any other, so your books stay right automatically.

## Good to know

- Terminals charge payments. Deposits keep working as they do today, charged upfront with the booking.
- Anything you add to the booking on the spot (add-ons, extra costs) is included in the open balance, ready to charge.
