# Payment terminals

Charge a booking right at the counter. A remaining balance, a walk-in, or a drink someone grabs on the way out: add it to the booking and tap it off on a card reader, so nobody gets sent off to pay somewhere else.

## Get a card reader

Order a reader from your payment provider:

- [Mollie card readers](https://www.mollie.com/products/pos-payments)
- [Stripe card readers](https://stripe.com/terminal/devices)

With Mollie you can also use Tap to Pay straight from your own phone, no extra hardware needed.

## Charge a booking

1. Open the booking and go to the Finance tab
2. Click Collect payment and pick Terminal
3. Check the amount and description, both pre-filled with what's open
4. Select your reader and hand it to the customer

You follow the payment live in the modal: connecting, waiting for the tap, paid. The payment lands on the booking like any other, so your books stay right automatically.

## Good to know

- Terminals charge payments. Deposits keep working as they do today: charged upfront and [returned from the booking](/guides/day-to-day/bookings/return-deposits-and-refunds).
- Anything you add to the booking on the spot (add-ons, extra costs) is included in the open balance, ready to charge.
