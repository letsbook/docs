# Search and filter bookings

The [bookings overview](https://dashboard.letsbook.app/bookings) is your operational home base. Bookings are grouped by pick-up day under headers like "Today" and "Tomorrow", filters sit in an always-visible rail on the left, and a scope switch above the list keeps you focused on what's ahead.

## Choosing your view

The scope control above the list decides which bookings you see:

- **Upcoming**: everything from today onward, with quick presets for All, Today, Next 7 days, and Next 30 days
- **Past**: finished bookings, with presets for the last 3, 7, or 30 days
- **Custom**: pick your own date range

## Finding bookings

There are two ways to quickly find a specific booking:

**From anywhere:** use the search field in the top of your [dashboard](https://dashboard.letsbook.app/) and start typing.

**On the bookings page:** use the Find booking field in the top bar. Search by customer name, email, phone number, or booking reference. Results appear as you type.

## Filtering bookings

On large screens the filters are always visible in the left rail. On smaller screens, click the filter icon in the top bar to open them. Filters apply instantly and combine, so "RIBs at the north dock that haven't paid" is three clicks away.

### Available filters

**Dock:** filter by pick-up location

**Boat model:** filter by boat type or model

**Status:** by default you see Confirmed and Completed bookings. Tick **Cancelled** to include those too, or switch to **Custom** to hand-pick from Draft, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled, and Expired

**Payment:** Unpaid, Partly paid, Paid, or Overpaid

**Documents:** waivers awaiting signing or completed

**Partner:** filter by [partner](/guides/settings/dive-deeper/partner-bookings)

**Labels:** filter using your custom booking labels

**Flagged:** show only bookings [flagged for attention](/guides/day-to-day/bookings/flag-bookings)

**Delivery issues:** show only bookings where an email or text [failed to arrive](/guides/day-to-day/bookings/booking-notifications)

Looking for bookings with add-ons? The rail links straight to the [order overview](https://dashboard.letsbook.app/orders), which is built for exactly that.

### Active filters

Active filters show as removable chips, so you always see exactly what's narrowing the list. Remove them one by one, or hit **Clear all** to reset the filters while keeping your search and date scope.

Your filters are reflected in the page URL, so refreshing keeps your view and sharing the link gives a colleague the exact same slice.
