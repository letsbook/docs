# Return deposits and refunds

Return a deposit, refund an overpayment, or settle a cancelled booking straight from the booking's Finance tab. No more detours through your payment provider's dashboard.

## The next-step card

Once a trip has ended (or the booking is cancelled), a next-step card appears on the Finance tab whenever money still needs to move. It tells you what's open and gives you one button to handle it: return the deposit, collect the balance, or refund an overpayment.

If the booking carries [flagged notes](/guides/day-to-day/bookings/flag-bookings), the card shows them right there, so you check the damage report before you return the deposit.

## Return a deposit

1. Open the booking and go to the Finance tab
2. Click Return deposit
3. Choose a full return, or switch to a partial return to withhold costs
4. Confirm

With a partial return you add cost lines for what you're keeping: $50 for that scratch, $25 for the fuel top-up. The customer gets the rest back, and both of you see exactly what was withheld and why.

The money returns through the original payment method. Your customer gets a notification, and the return shows up on the booking timeline.

## Refund an overpayment

Paid too much? The next-step card offers a refund for the difference. If a deposit is being returned at the same time, you can include the overpayment in one go.

## When the customer still owes you

If there's an open balance and you're holding a deposit, you can collect what's owed from the deposit and return the rest. One action, both sides settled.

## Cancelled bookings

For a cancelled booking with payments on it, the card offers a full refund. Add an internal note if you want to record why, it stays out of the customer's sight.

## Who can do this

Returning deposits and refunding requires the refund permission on your role. Set it under [roles and permissions](/guides/settings/collaborate/roles-permissions).
