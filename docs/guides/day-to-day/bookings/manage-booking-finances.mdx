# Booking finances

[Open booking](https://dashboard.letsbook.app/bookings) → **Finance tab**

The Finance tab shows your booking's financial overview in three sections: costs (left), received payments (right), and the balance at the top.

## Understanding the balance

The top bar shows the overall payment status:

- **$0.00 Paid in full** (green) = Customer paid everything
- **$X.XX Outstanding** (yellow) = Amount still owed
- **$X.XX Overpaid** (yellow) = Customer paid too much

The balance automatically updates when you add costs, discounts, or payments.

When there's an outstanding balance, the Collect payment button next to it opens one modal for every way to get paid. See [managing payments](#managing-payments).

## Managing costs

### Add a discount

Give discounts to adjust the booking total.

1. Click **Add discount** under Costs
2. Pick a preset from **Quick presets** to prefill the form, or fill it in by hand
3. Choose **Fixed amount** or **Percentage**
4. Enter description and value
5. Click **Save**

Discounts appear as negative line items under costs.

In **Percentage** mode, the form shows the live calculation (e.g. "10% of $300 = $30") before you save, so there's no mental math.

:::tip[Stop retyping the same discounts]
Save your recurring discounts as [presets](/guides/settings/presets) and apply them in one click.
:::

### Add extra costs

Charge for add-ons or damages that weren't part of the original booking.

1. Click **Add costs** under Costs
2. Pick a preset from **Quick presets** to prefill the form, or fill it in by hand
3. Choose **Fixed amount** or **Percentage**
4. Enter description and value
5. Select **Incl. tax** or **Excl. tax** and choose your tax rate
6. Click **Save**

The extra costs appear immediately and update the balance.

In **Percentage** mode, the form shows the live calculation (e.g. "15% of $300 = $45") against the booking total before you save.

:::tip[Recurring charges like cleaning or fuel?]
Save them as [cost presets](/guides/settings/presets) so the description, amount, and tax are one click away.
:::

:::info[Can't edit the rental price?]
The base rental price (boat + duration) is calculated automatically and can't be changed here. To adjust the rental price, edit the booking itself (change boat, duration, or dates). Use costs and discounts to adjust the total without changing the booking details.
:::

## Managing payments

### Collect a payment

One Collect payment button covers every way to settle an outstanding balance. Click it and the modal opens on the easiest method available for this customer, with the amount and description pre-filled. Switch methods at the top; whatever you filled in comes along.

**Charge the card on file.** Available when you've enabled Keep card on file in [integrations](https://dashboard.letsbook.app/integrations/psp) and the customer has a saved payment method. One confirmation and it's paid.

**Charge on a terminal.** Take the payment on your card reader while the customer is standing right there. See [payment terminals](/guides/settings/payments/terminals).

**Send a payment link.** Send it by email or text message (address and language pre-filled), or copy the link to share it anywhere. Set when the payment is due, and toggle a deposit onto the request if needed.

:::tip[When to use payment links]

- Collecting outstanding amount
- Charging for last-minute add-ons
- Requesting payment for manually added bookings
- After price changes from edits
  :::

### Add cash payment

Register payments made outside Let's Book (cash, bank transfer).

1. Click **Add cash payment** under Received
2. Enter the amount customer gave you
3. Click **Add**

The balance updates immediately. Cash payments don't go through payment providers.

## Managing deposits

### View deposits

Deposits appear at the bottom of the Finance tab, with their current balance. Once the trip has ended, return them straight from the booking: see [return deposits and refunds](/guides/day-to-day/bookings/return-deposits-and-refunds).

## Handle pricing changes

When you edit a booking and the price changes:

1. Check the new balance in Finance tab
2. If price increased → Collect the difference via [Collect payment](#collect-a-payment)
3. If price decreased → Refund the overpayment from the booking

### Process refunds

Refunds happen right on the booking. The next-step card on the Finance tab shows what's open and hands you the button, whether it's a deposit to return, an overpayment to refund, or a cancelled booking to settle.

See [return deposits and refunds](/guides/day-to-day/bookings/return-deposits-and-refunds) for the full flow.
